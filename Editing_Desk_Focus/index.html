How it works
⇩Import videos from Creator’s Desk, or add one.
▦Pipeline is a board: one row per video, all 12 stages as cells. Click any cell to jump to that stage.
◉Pick a stage node, set its status, add your own tasks and tap to tick them.
▶Start the timer on the stage you are editing. Stop when done.
◷Set a due date for the video and an estimate for each stage. The board flags what is due soon or late.
␣Space bar starts and stops the timer; the running time also shows in the browser tab. Left and right arrows move between stages, Page Up ticks the next task, Page Down unticks the last one.
⚑Markers and Rework logs feed the Issues tab.
Adjust time
Forgot to stop the timer? Remove the extra minutes, or set the exact total.
